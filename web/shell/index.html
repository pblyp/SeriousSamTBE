About the demos

The game files downloaded here are only those of the official Serious Sam: The First Encounter demo (2001) and Serious Sam: The Second Encounter demo (2001), which, to our knowledge, are freely redistributable. They are the same demos that were given away on magazine discs and download sites and that the Internet Archive hosts today. Game files from your own installations, your settings and your saved games stay on your device: they are never shared.
